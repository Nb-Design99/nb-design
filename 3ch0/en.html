One and the same shield

Coercive control cannot be fought on a single front. To protect people effectively and lastingly, technology, law and frontline social work must act as one and the same shield.

Challenge 01

Technological abuse

Digital tools and smartphones are exploited for surveillance, which significantly hinders survivors' access to help.

Challenge 02

Converging needs

An alert is not enough if it produces nothing before the judge. Life-saving protection must instantly seal the integrity of digital evidence, without depending on a compromised network.

Challenge 03

Coordinating stakeholders

No single stakeholder can act alone. Effectiveness relies on a direct bridge between social workers, advanced engineering, the police and the judicial authorities, to prevent cases being dropped.

04 — First project

SentrIQ: moving beyond the smartphone trap

Current emergency solutions rely almost exclusively on the victim's personal phone. That is precisely the object the abuser controls.

A.
SOS apps

Unusable under duress. They require unlocking the screen, opening an app, and leave visible traces in the history.

B.
Bluetooth alert buttons

Illusory range. Inoperative as soon as the phone is switched off, out of range or confiscated; easily detected by the abuser via Bluetooth settings.

C.
Manual recordings

Almost systematically rejected in court. Highly risky to trigger in a crisis, files easily challenged for tampering and inadmissible for lack of forensic sealing.

D.
Safety phones

Identifiable objects, vulnerable to physical destruction.

SentrIQ's technological architecture: the device's three pillars

01

Alert

A silent, autonomous alert that can be triggered under duress, entirely separate from the personal smartphone.

No smartphone required Silent
02

Transmission

A secure, sovereign transmission channel, operational even if the phone is confiscated, destroyed or spied on.

Sovereign Redundant
03

Evidence

Cryptographic sealing and certified timestamping, guaranteeing an unalterable, court-admissible chain of evidence.

Certified timestamping nFADP compliant

05 — Scope

From applied research to operational deployment

The association upholds the independence of ethical R&D, while the industrial phase relies on our startup and certified partners to guarantee sovereignty and manufacturing security in Switzerland.

Identifying the problem

Frontline diagnosis: identifying vital requirements with specialist associations, legal experts and medico-social professionals.

Fundamental and applied research

Interdisciplinary research: modelling protocols resistant to coercive control, with evidentiary sealing.

Development and prototypes

Hardware engineering: developing functional prototypes with HEIG-VD, 100% independent of the smartphone.

Validation and pilots

Real-world testing: technical and legal validation, followed by preparation of institutional pilot phases.

Production and operational deployment

Secure industrialisation led by our dedicated entity and certified industrial partners, guaranteeing sovereign manufacturing in Switzerland.

Research, prototyping, validation

06 — Division of roles

Scientific rigour and operational sovereignty

3CH0 carries the public interest, upstream research and the initial patent. The operating entity takes on the industrial legal obligations and public-sector deployment.

3CH0 — Research & public mission
  • Initial R&D risk — funding research and design costs with HEIG-VD.
  • Public interest & frontline — co-designing protocols with medico-social and judicial professionals, to guarantee the safety of the people being protected and the admissibility of evidence.
  • Development & prototypes — designing the first hardware and software architectures resistant to coercive control.
  • Ring-fenced intellectual property — filing and holding the seed patent within the association, guaranteeing its independence.
Operating entity — Sàrl & scaling up
  • Legal liability — assuming the legal framework and manufacturer's liability insurance required to deploy a safety tool in the field.
  • Manufacturing & certification — overseeing series industrial production and guaranteeing compliance with Swiss and European standards.
  • Institutional rollout — handling sales and contractual follow-up with public authorities, and coordinating the confidential handover of equipment through protective support networks.
  • Commercialising the innovation — a fair transfer of patent exploitation rights, whose proceeds go to the association and are reinvested in public-interest research. A share of the Sàrl's profits is also paid back to it every year to fund its frontline work.

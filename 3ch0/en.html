02 — The problem

76.8% of victims experience coercive control from someone close to them.

Swiss Federal Statistical Office, 2023 · domestic violence statistics

A multidimensional approach is essential

Coercive control cannot be tackled on a single front. Three challenges intersect, and none can be solved in isolation.

Challenge 01

Technological misuse

Digital tools and smartphones are exploited for surveillance, which makes it considerably harder for victims to access help.

Challenge 02

Converging needs

The response must combine immediate physical protection, communications cybersecurity and the preservation of evidence integrity.

Challenge 03

Coordination between stakeholders

Close synergy between frontline organisations, technologists and law enforcement has become essential.

04 — First project

SentrIQ, addressing the vulnerability of the smartphone

Current emergency solutions rely almost exclusively on the victim's personal phone. This is precisely the object the abuser controls.

A.
SOS apps

Require unlocking the phone — impossible under duress.

B.
Bluetooth buttons

Depend on a nearby smartphone, which is often confiscated.

C.
Manual recordings

Dangerous to trigger, easy to tamper with, legally fragile.

D.
Safety phones

Easily identifiable objects, vulnerable to physical destruction.

Three research areas

01

Protection

Designing a highly resilient, fully discreet alert, independent of personal devices.

No smartphone required Discreet
02

Infrastructure

Establishing communication that does not depend solely on the smartphone, and that survives its loss.

Redundancy Continuity
03

Data integrity

Exploring the security, timestamping and retention of evidence to guarantee its admissibility.

Timestamping FADP compliant

05 — Scope

Our mission ends where industrialisation begins

3CH0 focuses exclusively on the upstream phases of the innovation cycle. This is what guarantees its independence.

Identifying the problem

Vital frontline needs, defined together with experts and associations.

Fundamental and applied research

Interdisciplinary research to design new protection models.

Development and prototypes

Functional prototypes to test and validate technological hypotheses.

Validation and pilots

Scientific validation, followed by preparation for pilot deployments in the field.

Industrial handover

Beyond this stage, production and deployment pass to partners: licensing agreements, or a dedicated commercial entity.

Research, prototyping, validation

06 — Division of roles

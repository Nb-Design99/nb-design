01 — Mission

Giving survivors the means to be heard

Technology research, legal expertise and frontline knowledge, serving a single aim: raising the alarm and documenting without ever exposing people further.

I — Protect

Trigger discreet assistance at the critical moment. No screen, no sound, no visible gesture.

II — Document

Preserve timestamped, sealed material from the moment of capture, protected against any alteration.

III — Establish

Provide the authorities with evidence that is technically and legally admissible.

Behind closed doors, the blind spot of evidence

03 — Vulnerability

The smartphone is the point of failure

The lifeline is also the object most closely watched, seized and controlled by the abuser. Every current solution depends on it.

A.
SOS apps

Require unlocking the phone — impossible under duress.

B.
Bluetooth buttons

Depend on the smartphone being nearby, and it is often confiscated.

C.
Manual recordings

Dangerous to start, easy to tamper with, legally fragile.

D.
Safety phones

Visible objects, vulnerable to physical destruction.

50–75%

of intimate partner violence cases involve coercive control

Continuous digital monitoring, known passwords, shared location. Any app installed on the phone is visible — and therefore dangerous. Protection must shift its centre of gravity.

04 — Technology

Three pillars, one chain of trust

From the home to a Swiss sovereign cloud. Security & privacy by design, end to end.

01

Resilient alert

A micro-device decoupled from any personal equipment. Silent trigger, haptic confirmation, no visual feedback.

Smartphone-free Silent
02

Autonomous infrastructure

A mesh network of concealed relays inside the home. If one node is destroyed, the others take over. Built-in batteries and cellular fallback.

Mesh network Redundant
03

Forensic integrity

Certified timestamping, cryptographic signature generated at hardware level, asymmetric encryption and sealing at capture.

Hardware sealing FADP compliant

05 — Chain of custody

From alert to admissible evidence

Courts require authenticated, unaltered evidence. Every step is secured automatically.

Targeted capture

Triggered only by the person under protection. No continuous surveillance.

Immediate sealing

Timestamp and cryptographic signature generated at hardware level, at source.

Encrypted transmission

Routed through the relays to a Swiss sovereign cloud, bypassing personal devices.

Controlled access

Data inaccessible without the explicit authorisation of the judicial authorities.

Full traceability

The data life cycle documented in a tamper-proof record, in line with the FADP.

Sealing at hardware level
